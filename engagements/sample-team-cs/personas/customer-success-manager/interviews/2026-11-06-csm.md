## Persona answers

### P1. Your role

"I'm a customer success manager on the customer success team. I work in Claude in the browser and in Slack, on my own laptop; nobody here has a company-managed machine."

### P2. Who you serve

"Customer success managers like me. Each of us looks after about 40 accounts."

### P3. A good week

"I walk into every call already knowing the account, and the follow-up is out the same afternoon. The jobs: brief me before a call, draft the follow-up and log the call, draft renewal quotes, flag renewal risk and pull the tickets together when something is escalated."

### P4. How it should sound

"Warm and direct with customers. With the team, brief and factual."

### P5. Never, and ask first

"Never send an email to a customer itself. I check and send. Never quote a price that isn't on the HubSpot deal."

### P6. Who you turn to

"My team lead. Anything about a contract goes to our finance and legal lead."

## Task block

Task id: C1    Task name: Account brief before a customer call

#### F1. Your tasks

"About six calls a week, half an hour of prep each. All eight of us on the team do it. This is the first thing I'd pilot. I want it to brief me on the account before each call, so I walk into the call already knowing the account."

#### F2. What goes in, what comes out

"HubSpot for the company, contacts, deals, open tickets and notes. Google Drive for the account plan, the last QBR deck and the contract. I only look at those. Out comes a one-page brief. Contracts and CRM data are confidential."

#### F3. What good looks like

"I can share two good briefs and one weak one from our 'CS examples' drive. Our team lead, Marisol Quenby, is who I'd ask whether a brief is right."

#### F4. If it goes wrong

"Medium. A wrong renewal date or contact name in front of the customer."

#### F5. What starts it

"I do, before the call."

#### F6. Who else sees it

"Just me and sometimes my team lead. It stays inside the company."

#### F7. Undo and money

"It's a brief; I can throw it away. No money."

#### F8. Things written by outsiders

"No. It's our CRM and our own documents."

#### F9. Remembering

"Yes, I'd like it to remember my accounts and how I like the brief laid out."

## Task block

Task id: C4    Task name: Follow-up email after a customer call, and a call note in HubSpot

#### F1. Your tasks

"After the same six calls a week, about 25 minutes each. All eight of us on the team do it. Pilot this with the brief. I want it to draft the follow-up email and the call note for HubSpot, so the follow-up is out the same afternoon."

#### F2. What goes in, what comes out

"My call notes or the transcript in Google Drive, which I only look at. I want a draft follow-up email I check and send myself, and a call note created on the HubSpot company record. It's customer call material, so confidential."

#### F3. What good looks like

"Two good follow-ups and one that went wrong, from the examples drive. Marisol Quenby, our team lead, is who I'd ask whether a follow-up is right."

#### F4. If it goes wrong

"Medium. A wrong commitment in the email, or the note on the wrong record."

#### F5. What starts it

"Me, after the call."

#### F6. Who else sees it

"The customer gets the email once I send it. The team sees the HubSpot note."

#### F7. Undo and money

"The draft can be thrown away, and the note can be fixed in HubSpot. I want to approve the note before it's created. No money."

#### F8. Things written by outsiders

"My own notes. No."

#### F9. Remembering

"Same as the brief."
