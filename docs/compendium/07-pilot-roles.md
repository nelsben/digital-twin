# 07 — The pilot roles

These task lists are **examples to test with real people in each role.** We haven't validated them yet. Before writing any skill, interview two or three people per role: which tasks eat their week, and what "good" looks like.

## Shared skills (one common plugin for every role)

| Skill | Why it's shared |
|---|---|
| Writing in house style | Every role writes to customers or colleagues |
| Account research brief | Sales, CS and finance all need "what's going on with this account" |
| Meeting prep and follow-up | Same shape for every role: agenda, context, actions, follow-up |
| Weekly status report | Every role reports up |
| Finding internal knowledge | Policy and process questions |

Building these once keeps each role plugin small. That helps skills trigger reliably, since the more skills are loaded, the less reliably the right one fires. It also gives every role the same quality.

## By role

| | Sales / go-to-market | Customer success / support | Operations / finance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Typical skills** | Discovery-call prep; updating CRM after a call; first drafts of proposals; competitor battlecards; pipeline summary | Account-health review; QBR prep; ticket triage and suggested replies; renewal-risk summary | Assembling recurring reports; reconciliation checks; intake and routing of requests; answering policy questions |
| **Connectors likely needed** | CRM, email and calendar, call recordings, docs | CRM, ticketing, product usage data, docs | ERP or finance system, spreadsheets and BI, ticketing or intake, docs |
| **Starting autonomy** | Level 1 (drafts); CRM writes at level 2 | Level 1; ticket tagging may reach level 3 | Level 1; numbers stay at level 1 or 2 |
| **Likely first hosted job** | Monday pipeline digest in the team channel | Nightly scan for at-risk accounts | Month-end checklist status |
| **Biggest risk** | Wrong facts going to a customer | Over-confident replies on tickets | Wrong numbers that look plausible |
| **Freedom level in skills** | High (judgment) | Medium (templates) | Low (exact scripts for calculations) |

## What to capture in role interviews

For each candidate task:

- **Time:** how often it happens and how long it takes.
- **Inputs:** which systems and documents it uses.
- **Output and audience:** what it produces, and who sees it (internal or external).
- **Cost of a mistake:** what happens if it goes wrong.
- **Good and bad examples.** These become the eval cases ([03](03-skills-authoring.md)).
