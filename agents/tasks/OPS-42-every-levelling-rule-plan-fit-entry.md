## Why

The second adversarial review read all 99 levelling rules, 17 plan-fit entries and three industry packs against the compendium sections they cite and found 31 rules, 6 plan-fit entries and 4 pack entries that are stricter, looser or more specific than the page, or carry the wrong basis or unverified label. Eleven lead to a decision a consultant would act on wrongly (a Claude Tag routine under ZDR, a lost level-2 cap, no settings baseline for highly sensitive work outside private equity). The workflow's promise is a correctly levelled agent with a true source for each decision, so each row is fixed here without changing the compendium.
