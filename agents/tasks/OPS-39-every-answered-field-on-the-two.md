## Why

The two fictional persona cards are the worked examples a consultant copies, and the workflow's promise is that every field traces to a source. A reviewer confirmed that several entries marked answered cite an interview answer that does not say it, or a question the field catalogue does not list for that field. The backlog, dashboard and two more skills are generated from these cards next, so the cards are corrected first. The persona card is card.json (not the persona voice file in starter-kit/templates/persona, and not a task card in agents/tasks).
