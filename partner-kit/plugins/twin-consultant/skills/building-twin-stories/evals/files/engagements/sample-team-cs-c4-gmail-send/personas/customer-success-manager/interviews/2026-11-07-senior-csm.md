## Task block

Task id: C2    Task name: Weekly renewal-risk digest

#### F1. Your tasks

"Once a week, about an hour and a half across the team. All eight of us use it. Not in the pilot. I want it to flag the accounts whose renewal is at risk each week, so no renewal is missed for lack of warning."

#### F2. What goes in, what comes out

"HubSpot renewal dates, deal stage and contacts, plus the usage trend per account from our product-usage database; I only look at those. One post in the team's Slack channel. Confidential."

#### F3. What good looks like

"Two good digests and one that missed an account. Marisol Quenby is who I'd ask whether a digest is right."

#### F4. If it goes wrong

"Medium. An at-risk account gets missed, or a healthy one gets flagged."

#### F5. What starts it

"A schedule, Monday morning, for the whole team."

#### F6. Who else sees it

"The team, in the channel. All inside the company."

#### F7. Undo and money

"A post can be deleted. No money."

#### F8. Things written by outsiders

"The usage data comes from what customers do in the product, so yes, treat it as outside input."

#### F9. Remembering

"Remembering each manager's accounts would help, and how they like their briefs laid out."
